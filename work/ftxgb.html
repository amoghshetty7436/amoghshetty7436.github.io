FTXGB-Net: aerosols from orbit

Air quality across India depends on fine particles that satellites can only see indirectly. Our team built a model that reads MODIS Terra observations and retrieves four aerosol properties at once, more accurately than the operational NASA products.

The approach

The FT-Transformer learns rich representations of mixed tabular inputs: satellite reflectances, weather and terrain. XGBoost then makes the final retrievals from those features. The hybrid predicts all four properties together instead of training one model per target.

We validated against AERONET ground stations with both site-based and time-based splits, so the model is tested on places and periods it never saw. It beat MODIS MAIAC and MOD04_3K on RMSE.
